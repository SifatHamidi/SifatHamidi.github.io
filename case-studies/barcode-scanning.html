Measurement & Barcode Scanning in Odoo 17

Bringing laser measurement and fast camera scanning straight into Odoo's Barcode app, so warehouse operators capture data on the floor instead of typing it in.

Platform
Odoo 17
Area
Barcode · Inventory
Stack
OWL.js · Python

The problem

Operators handling long stock items, such as pipes, had to measure each item by hand and key lengths and barcodes into transfers one by one. It was slow, it depended on dedicated scanner hardware, and every manual entry was a chance for a typo to end up in stock records.

The approach

Both inputs feed one custom module inside the standard Barcode app.

  • Laser measurement in the flow. A Bluetooth laser measuring device connects to the Barcode app, so a measured length goes straight into the current operation — no reading off a display and typing it in.
  • Any device becomes a scanner. Camera-based scanning with the Dynamsoft SDK lets operators scan with a phone or tablet camera instead of dedicated hardware.
  • Continuous multi-scan on transfers. A dedicated barcode entry screen keeps the camera running so operators can scan item after item on a transfer without stopping between scans.
  • Documented for the client. Client-facing documentation explains each feature so operators and managers can adopt it quickly.

Key decisions

Extend the standard Barcode app, don't replace it

Building inside Odoo's own Barcode app with OWL keeps the familiar operator flow and standard stock logic, and keeps the customization smaller and easier to maintain.

Use a proven scanning SDK

Phone cameras struggle with worn labels and poor lighting. A dedicated SDK gives fast, reliable decoding instead of a fragile home-grown scanner.

Design for continuous work

Operators scan in bulk. A screen built for scanning many items in a row, with clear feedback after each scan, matches how the work actually happens on the floor.

Outcome

Measuring and scanning now happen in one place inside Odoo. Operators enter far less by hand, entry errors drop, and transfers are tallied faster using everyday phones and tablets.
